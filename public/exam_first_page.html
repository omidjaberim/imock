Quick Overview (IELTS-style)

Listening
Duration: ~30–40 minutes (including transfer time). 4 sections — conversations and monologues. Focus on: keywords, synonyms, and note-taking.
Reading
Duration: 60 minutes. 3 long passages, different question types (multiple choice, matching, True/False/Not Given). Skim, scan, and manage time per passage.
Writing
Duration: 60 minutes. Task 1: describe data (chart/table) — 20 mins. Task 2: essay — 40 mins. Plan, write clear paragraphs, answer the prompt fully.
Speaking
Duration: 11–14 minutes. Short interview, cue-card, discussion. Speak clearly and develop answers with examples.

Top tips

  • Read instructions for each question carefully — small differences matter.
  • Time management: keep an eye on the section timer and move on if a question is taking too long.
  • For Listening & Reading, underline or take short notes; for Writing, spend 5–10 minutes planning your Task 2 essay.
  • Use clear structure: intro, 2–3 paragraphs, conclusion for essays.
  • Speak naturally in the Speaking section — fluency and coherence count as much as vocabulary.
